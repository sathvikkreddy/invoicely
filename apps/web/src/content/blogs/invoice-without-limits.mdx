# Invoice Without Limits: Customizable and Account-Backed

Invoicely.gg keeps the invoice workflow focused: enter the work you completed, apply your branding, preview the result, and export a polished PDF or PNG.

## Your invoices follow your account

Sign in with Google to use the dashboard and invoice editor. Invoices and reusable sender or client details are saved in the application database, so you can return later to edit, duplicate, update the payment status, or download them again.

Logos and signatures are stored in the account asset library. Upload each asset once, then select it while creating future invoices.

## Customize the details that matter

- Choose an invoice template, font, color, and currency.
- Add line items, billing adjustments, payment information, notes, and terms.
- Save default company and client details to prefill new invoices.
- Reuse an existing invoice as the starting point for a new one.
- Export the finished invoice as PDF or PNG.

## Built in the open

Invoicely.gg is open source. You can inspect the implementation, contribute improvements, or self-host it with PostgreSQL, Better Auth, and Cloudflare R2.

The hosted application requires an internet connection and an authenticated account because invoice records and assets are stored on the server. That design provides a consistent saved workspace instead of tying business records to a single browser.

## Get started

1. [Sign in and create an invoice](https://invoicely.sathvikkreddy.xyz/create/invoice).
2. Add your company, client, and line-item details.
3. Preview the document and save or download it.
4. Return to the dashboard whenever you need to manage it.

Your invoice history, reusable details, and brand assets are ready the next time you sign in.
