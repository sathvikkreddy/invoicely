# Why Choose Invoicely.gg for Professional Invoices?

Invoice software should make routine billing quick without hiding the document behind a complicated workflow. Invoicely.gg provides a focused editor, a live preview, and an authenticated dashboard for managing saved invoices.

![Invoice platform comparison](/blogs/why-choose-invoicely/comparison-image.png)

## A complete invoice workflow

- Create and customize invoices in one editor.
- Save records to your account and continue editing later.
- Track pending, paid, expired, refunded, and error states.
- Review billed, outstanding, overdue, and collected totals on the dashboard.
- Export client-ready PDF and PNG files.

## Reuse instead of retyping

Save default company and client details to prefill a new invoice. Upload logos and signatures to the asset library, then select them from the editor. You can also import a previous invoice into the form when a new job resembles an earlier one.

## Transparent technology

The project is open source, so its implementation can be reviewed and self-hosted. The hosted app uses Google sign-in, PostgreSQL for invoice data and saved defaults, and Cloudflare R2 for logos and signatures.

## A straightforward choice

Invoicely.gg is a good fit when you want a purpose-built invoice editor, account-backed history, professional document exports, and code you can inspect. Because records are stored with your account, the hosted application requires sign-in and an internet connection.
