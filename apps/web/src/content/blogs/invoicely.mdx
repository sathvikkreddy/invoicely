# Discover Invoicely.gg: An Open-Source Invoice Workspace

An invoice records the services or products delivered, the amount due, payment terms, and the businesses involved. Invoicely.gg helps freelancers and small teams create that record, save it, and export a professional document from one focused workspace.

## Create, save, and revisit invoices

After signing in with Google, you can create an invoice with your company details, client information, line items, billing adjustments, notes, and payment instructions. The editor includes a live preview and supports PDF and PNG exports.

Invoices are stored in PostgreSQL and connected to your account. The invoice list and financial dashboard make it possible to revisit a record, update its status, or edit it later without relying on one browser or device.

## Keep reusable details ready

The asset library stores logos and signatures in Cloudflare R2. It also lets you save default company and client details, which prefill the next invoice you create. These account-backed defaults reduce repetitive entry while remaining editable for each document.

![Account-backed invoice workflow](/blogs/invoicely/privacy-image.png)

## Open-source transparency

Invoicely.gg is open source. Developers can inspect how authentication, invoice persistence, asset storage, PDF generation, and deployment work, contribute changes, or operate their own instance.

The hosted application requires sign-in and an internet connection. Access to dashboard records is session-protected, and server mutations validate input and verify ownership before changing invoice or asset data.
