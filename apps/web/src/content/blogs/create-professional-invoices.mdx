# Create Professional Invoices in 3 Simple Steps

A clear invoice helps a client understand who they are paying, what the charge covers, and when payment is due. Invoicely.gg brings those details into one editor with a live preview.

![Simple invoice process](/blogs/create-professional-invoices/process-image.png)

## Step 1: Sign in and add your branding

Sign in with Google, then open the invoice creator. Select a template, font, and theme color. You can upload a logo or signature to your account asset library and reuse it on later invoices.

If you invoice with the same business or client details often, save them as defaults from the Assets page. New invoices will start with those values already filled in.

## Step 2: Enter the invoice details

Add your company and client information, then define:

- the invoice prefix and serial number;
- issue and due dates;
- currency and payment terms;
- item descriptions, quantities, and unit prices;
- taxes, discounts, notes, terms, and payment instructions.

The preview updates as you work so you can check the finished layout before sending it.

## Step 3: Save and export

Save the invoice to your account, or download it as a PDF or PNG. Saved invoices appear in the invoice list and dashboard, where you can edit them or update their status later.

For a similar job, use Import in the editor to copy the fields from one of your existing invoices and adjust only what changed.

## Before you send it

- Confirm the client name and billing address.
- Use specific line-item descriptions.
- Check the currency, dates, totals, and payment instructions.
- Preview the exported layout and proofread the notes.
